MI-SPACE — NSCLC Multiplex Immunofluorescence Atlas
Cell-type co-localization, tissue niches and outcome association in 153 resected tumors

Abstract
An interactive portal over the public Vectra 3 lung cohort that the BayesRx / MI-SPACE spatial tools are demonstrated on. Skip this card and you land on the Bench, its own mode beside Live and Portal, where four of the group's own methods are run on these 153 patients: DIMPLE and CoMPAdRe from the authors' packages, SPARTIN measured and then declined, and the plain-LDA baseline underneath TopSpace. Behind it sits the portal those methods are benchmarked against: cell-type composition, pairwise co-localization measured against a label-permutation null, eight cellular-neighborhood niches and the full outcome screen, every number recomputed from the deposited cell table rather than quoted from a publication.

Analysis & reproducibility

Objects were segmented and phenotyped with Akoya inForm before deposition; this portal consumes those calls rather than re-segmenting. Everything downstream is ours: KD-tree cross-G at 15/30/50/75 µm, a 20× label-permutation null at 30 µm, k-means niches on 30 µm neighborhood composition (k = 8, seed 0), Cox and log-rank with Benjamini-Hochberg FDR across each full family. The Methods tab states every statistic and its limits.

Integrated analysis tools
Live Walkthrough
Ten narrated scenes on a particle canvas. Scene 6 shuffles the cell-type labels on screen so you watch the permutation null happen.
Cohort
Composition over all 1.6M cells, the phenotyping check against each channel, and the full 153-patient table.
Tissue Map
Per-field cell maps at real coordinates, faceted one panel per cell type, or colored by niche, compartment or channel.
Cellular Niches
Eight neighborhoods from tumor core to B-cell aggregate, recovering the pathologist's compartment without being shown it.
Co-localization
ΔG for all 36 ordered cell-type pairs against the permutation null, with raw G alongside and G(r) curves by radius.
Outcome Screen
Cox per 1 SD and Kaplan–Meier for every feature, with the clinical strata as a positive control.
Group Contrasts
Mann-Whitney with FDR across MHC-II status, stage, sex and vital status.
Methods Bench — its own mode
Four of the group's methods on these fields. DIMPLE installed and run on all 761, including its own lm_dist inference. CoMPAdRe run from the authors' code, selecting across correlated spatial features. SPARTIN run, measured for reproducibility, and declined. TopSpace's plain-LDA baseline, since TopSpace has no public implementation. Where a method beats ours, or ours beats it, is stated rather than hidden.
Moveable Panels
Drag a gutter to resize, collapse a side panel, or drag a panel's grip onto another to swap them. The layout persists.
How to use
1. Watch first — the portal opens on Live. Press play, or click any scene in the left rail to jump.
2. Switch to Portal — the header toggle, or the button on the last scene.
3. Pick a patient — the left rail filters by MHC-II status, stage and sex; the right panel carries that patient's composition and strongest co-localization pairs.
4. Read ΔG, not G — on Interactions, blue is co-localized beyond chance and red is segregated. Raw G sits next to it so the correction is auditable.
5. Check the negative — Outcomes shows the whole screen, including the fact that none of it is prognostic, and the permutation calibration proving the screen was working.
6. Start at the Bench — it is its own mode, beside Live and Portal in the header, and it is where skipping this card lands you. Switch between the four methods at the top: DIMPLE reproduces its published finding, CoMPAdRe and DIMPLE independently land on the same MHC-II pair, SPARTIN is measured and declined, and TopSpace's baseline degenerates. Drag DIMPLE's bandwidth and watch the intensity surfaces and the code listing follow it.
Provenance & caveats
An independent re-analysis of a public deposit, not a reproduction.
  • The panel carries no CD4 antibody; the depositor's "CD4 T" call is a derived CD3+CD8− gate, inherited here as-is.
  • "Other" is 19.3% of cells and is not a cell type — a low-signal inForm catch-all, dim on every channel including DAPI. It is drawn in neutral grey for that reason.
  • Co-localization is reported as ΔG against a 20× label-permutation null, never raw proximity. Tumor→tumor G is 0.98 at 30 µm, near the ceiling, so its ΔG is compressed.
  • No spatial feature is prognostic here — 0 of 61 at FDR < 0.10 for overall survival. The screen is calibrated: against 1,000 outcome permutations the observed 2 hits at raw p < 0.05 sit on the null median and the strongest has a family-wise p of 0.40. Clinical MHC-II status is prognostic (5-year p = 0.047), so the endpoint is the constraint rather than the imaging. Both are on the Outcomes tab.
  • Our ΔG is outperformed by DIMPLE's JSD on the MHC-II axis — the permutation null conditions on the point pattern and absorbs part of the infiltration signal (ΔG p = 0.33 versus JSD p = 0.0002). The Bench tab reports this against ourselves rather than omitting it.
